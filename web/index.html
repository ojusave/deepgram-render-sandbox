Ask about the sample, or upload a spreadsheet saved as a CSV (.csv) file. Speak naturally or type. Your answer, chart, and downloadable table appear together.

Deepgram understands your question and writes a small program to answer it. Render runs that program in a temporary, separate computer workspace called a sandbox. It closes after the calculation. Follow each step above the conversation; Activity keeps the details.

Use sample data. Audio and questions are sent to Deepgram and its language-model provider. Column names and five example rows help Deepgram understand your file; the complete file is processed on Render. This demo keeps your data temporarily for up to one hour without activity. Restarting the app clears it.

CSV only, up to 500 KB and 10,000 rows. Each conversation lasts up to five minutes.
